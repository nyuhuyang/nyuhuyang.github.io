The Feynman Technique

“I learned very early the difference between knowing the name of something and knowing something.” — Richard P. Feynman, What Do You Care What Other People Think?

Knowing terminology is not the same as understanding. Explain the idea in plain language, identify the gaps, return to the source, and reconstruct it until you can apply it in a new context.
